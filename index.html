Carina Apps

A small suite of minimal Android apps from Carina Apps. They are inexpensive, have no ads, and keep gaining features. Your data stays on your device unless you turn on an optional feature that a privacy policy describes. Several apps never request internet access. The others use it only for a specific job: fetching forecasts, processing an optional Pro purchase through Google Play, or — if you turn it on — encrypted desktop sync through your own Google Drive.

Carina Tasks for desktop

A Windows and Linux app that can sync with Carina Tasks on your phone. Sync is optional, encrypted with a passphrase only you know, and uses your own Google Drive. Carina Apps never sees your tasks. Download Carina Tasks for desktop.
