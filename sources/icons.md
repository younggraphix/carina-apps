# Carina Icons Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 24, 2026_

Carina Icons (`com.carina.icons`) is an icon pack published by Carina Apps. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

## The short version

Carina Icons collects nothing. It has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts, and it does not have permission to use the internet.

## What the app accesses

- **Your list of installed apps.** The Request screen looks at which launchable apps are installed on your phone so it can show the ones that don't have a Carina icon yet. This list is read on your device, shown to you, and never stored or sent anywhere by the app.
- **Your launcher.** When you tap Apply, Carina Icons asks your launcher (for example Nova, Lawnchair, Niagara, Smart Launcher or Carina Launcher) to use the icon pack. Launchers read the pack's icons directly from the app.

## Icon requests

If you choose to request icons, Carina Icons opens your own email app with a pre-filled message listing the apps you selected (their names and Android activity names). Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. If you send it, we use the email only to add icons, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

## Data sharing and retention

The app does not collect, store, share or sell personal data. There is nothing to delete because nothing is kept.

## Children

Carina Icons is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with an app update and the date above will change.
