# Carina Focus — Privacy Policy

_Effective November 24, 2026 · Carina Apps_

Carina Focus is a Pomodoro focus timer with optional app blocking and Do Not Disturb. It is built to work entirely on your phone.

## The short version

**Carina Focus does not collect, store on a server, share, or sell any personal data.** There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send anything over the network.

## What stays on your device

- **Your focus history**: for each focus round, its start and end time and how many minutes you focused. This powers the Stats screen (sessions per day and week, total focus time, streaks).
- **Your settings**: timer lengths, rounds, sounds, vibration, theme, accent, and the list of apps you chose to block.
- If you use Android's own device backup, Android may include this data in your personal backup under your Google account settings. Carina Apps has no access to that backup.
- Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes this data.

## App blocking (optional)

If you turn on app blocking, Carina Focus asks for two Android special accesses, and explains them in the app first:

- **Usage access.** While a focus round is running, the app checks about once a second which app is on screen, so it can tell when you open an app you chose to block. Only the name of the app on screen is read. It is used immediately and is **not stored, logged or sent anywhere**. The app does not read what you do inside other apps.
- **Display over other apps.** Lets the "Back to focus" page appear on top of a blocked app.

The check only runs during a focus round you started. It stops during breaks, when paused, and when the timer ends. You can turn both accesses off at any time in Android Settings, and Carina Focus keeps working as a plain timer.

To show the list of apps you can block, the app asks Android for the apps that appear in your launcher. That list stays on your device.

## Permissions and why they're used

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | Shows the running timer with Pause, Skip and Stop while the app is in the background. |
| Foreground service (special use) | Keeps a focus session you started counting accurately, and keeps app blocking working, while you're in another app or the screen is off. |
| Wake lock | Keeps the processor awake so a round ends on time with the screen off. |
| Vibrate | Optional vibration when a focus round or break starts. |
| Do Not Disturb access (optional) | Turns on Do Not Disturb (priority only) during focus rounds and puts your previous mode back afterwards. |
| Usage access (optional) | App blocking: see which app is on screen during a focus round. See above. |
| Display over other apps (optional) | App blocking: show the "Back to focus" page over a blocked app. |

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
