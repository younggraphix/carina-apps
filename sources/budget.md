# Carina Budget — Privacy Policy

_Effective November 26, 2026 · Carina Apps_

Carina Budget is a personal budget and expense tracker. It is built to work entirely on your phone.

## The short version

**Carina Budget does not collect, store on a server, share, or sell any personal data.** There are no accounts, no bank connections, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app itself collects no data. The only network use is Google Play Billing for the optional Pro purchase (see below).

## What stays on your device

- **Your entries, categories, budgets, recurring items and settings** (amounts, notes, dates, currency, theme and accent) are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device.
- This data never leaves your device through the app. The app doesn't send it to Carina Apps or anyone else.
- **Bank statement import:** when you import a statement or transaction history (CSV, OFX/QFX or QIF), the app reads only the file you pick in Android's file picker, on your device. The app keeps the transactions you choose to import, plus each line's payee text and a short reference used to spot duplicates. It also keeps the category rules you save and the column layout of CSV files you've matched. The statement file itself is not copied or uploaded, and the app never connects to your bank.
- **Export and import** happen only when you choose them. You pick where the CSV or JSON file is saved (or which file to read) with Android's file picker. Carina Apps never sees those files. Where they go afterwards (for example a cloud drive you chose) is up to you and that service.
- If you use Android's own device backup, Android may include the app's local data in your personal backup under your Google account settings. Carina Apps has no access to that backup.
- Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes this data.

## Optional Pro purchase (Google Play Billing)

Carina Budget is free to use. An optional one-time Carina Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play. When you buy or restore Pro, **Google Play Billing** handles the purchase: Google processes the payment and keeps your payment details and purchase record under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app only asks Google Play whether Pro has been bought, and saves a yes/no on your device so Pro keeps working offline.

This is the only reason the app has the Internet permission (Google Play Billing adds it). The app itself collects no data and never sends your content anywhere. Carina Apps never receives your card or payment details. Google Play gives us the standard order record for a sale (such as the order number, date, price and country), as it does for every Play purchase.

## Permissions

The only permissions are Internet and Google Play billing, both added by Google Play Billing and used only for the optional Pro purchase. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, SMS, bank accounts or any other app's data.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
