Carina Launcher – Privacy Policy

Effective: November 15, 2026 · App: Carina Launcher (Android, package com.carina.launcher)

Carina Launcher is a home-screen app. It is built to work on your device: it has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking SDKs, and no server of our own. It does not even request Android's Internet permission, so it cannot send anything over the network. We (the developer) never receive, store, sell, or share your personal data. This policy explains exactly what the app touches and why.

Installed apps

As a launcher, Carina needs to see every app you have installed so it can list and open them. The list (app names, icons, package names) is read on demand by the device, used only to draw the home screen, drawer, search, icon-pack and widget pickers, and is never transmitted anywhere.

Calendar widget

If you add the Calendar widget and grant calendar access, the app reads upcoming events from your device's calendar to display them. Events are never copied off the device or sent anywhere. Revoke access any time in Android Settings.

Barcode / Image widget

When you pick an image with the system photo picker, the app gets access to only that one image, copies it into its private storage (optionally trimming white borders), and shows it on your home screen. The copy is deleted when you remove the widget. Images are never uploaded.

Other permissions

Carina does not request location or Internet access. The only other permissions it uses are:

  • Expand status bar – lets a swipe down on the home screen open the notification shade.
  • Request delete packages – lets the "Uninstall" menu item open Android's own uninstall confirmation.

Widgets from other apps

Carina can host widgets made by other apps you have installed (for example a weather or music widget). Those widgets are run and updated by the app that made them, under that app's own permissions and privacy policy; Carina only gives them space on your home screen.

Backups

If you have Android device backup turned on, Android may include Carina's local settings and layout in your device backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account's backup settings. We never have access to it.

Children

Carina Launcher is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your choices and deleting data

All data lives on your device. Clearing the app's storage (Android Settings › Apps › Carina Launcher › Storage › Clear storage) or uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.
