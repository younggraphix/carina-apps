Carina Timer — Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026 · Carina Apps

Carina Timer is an interval, workout and stopwatch timer. It is built to work entirely on your phone.

The short version

Carina Timer does not collect, store on a server, share, or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your saved timers and settings (names, durations, rounds, theme, accent and cue preferences) are stored locally in the app’s private storage on your device.
  • This data never leaves your device through the app. The app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send anything over the network.
  • If you use Android’s own device backup, Android may include the app’s local data in your personal backup under your Google account settings. Carina Apps has no access to that backup.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes this data.

Permissions and why they’re used

Permission Why
Notifications Shows the running timer with Pause, Skip and Stop while the app is in the background.
Foreground service (health) Keeps a workout timer counting and cueing accurately while you’re in another app or the screen is off.
High sampling rate sensors Required by Android for the “health” foreground-service type. The app does not read any sensors.
Wake lock Keeps the processor awake so interval cues fire on time with the screen off.
Vibrate Vibration cues at interval changes and the 3-2-1 countdown.

Voice cues use your phone’s built-in text-to-speech engine on the device. Carina Timer sends no text or audio anywhere.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
