Carina Habits — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Carina Habits is published by Carina Apps. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

The short version

Carina Habits does not collect, share, or sell any data. Everything you enter stays on your phone. The only network use is Google Play Billing for the optional Pro purchase.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your habits, check-ins, reminder times and settings are saved only on your device, in the app’s private storage.
  • The app never sends your habits or anything else off your device. Its Internet permission is added by Google Play Billing and is used only for the optional Pro purchase.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • There is no account. We don’t know who you are.

Optional Pro purchase (Google Play Billing)

Carina Habits is free to use. An optional one-time Carina Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play. When you buy or restore Pro, Google Play Billing handles the purchase: Google processes the payment and keeps your payment details and purchase record under the Google Privacy Policy. The app only asks Google Play whether Pro has been bought, and saves a yes/no on your device so Pro keeps working offline.

This is the only reason the app has the Internet permission (Google Play Billing adds it). The app itself collects no data and never sends your content anywhere. Carina Apps never receives your card or payment details. Google Play gives us the standard order record for a sale (such as the order number, date, price and country), as it does for every Play purchase.

Exports and backups

  • Export JSON writes a file to a location you choose (for example your Downloads folder or a cloud drive you pick). After that, the file is yours, and whatever service you saved it to handles it under its own policy.
  • Import JSON reads only the file you pick.
  • If you have Android’s own device backup turned on, Android may include the app’s data in your Google backup. Google handles that backup; Carina Apps never sees it. You can turn it off in your phone’s settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the habit reminders you set up. Optional.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): so reminders arrive at the exact time you chose. Optional; without it reminders may be a few minutes late.
  • Run at startup: to set your reminders again after your phone restarts.
  • Internet and Google Play billing: added by Google Play Billing, used only for the optional Pro purchase.

Children

The app doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete habits in the app, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. All data is removed from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
