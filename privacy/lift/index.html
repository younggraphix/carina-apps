Lift Tracker – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026 (updated for version 1.0.4, Health Connect sync) · Developer: Carina Apps

Lift Tracker is a workout log for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: we do not collect any data. Optional Health Connect sync keeps your data on your phone.

Information we collect

None. Lift Tracker has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting services and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device.

Information stored on your device

The workouts, routines, exercises, body-weight entries and settings you enter are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. We never receive them. You can delete them at any time by deleting individual entries in the app, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

Backups and exports

If you choose Back up to file or Export CSV, the app writes a file to a location you pick using Android's file picker. Where that file goes afterwards (for example your phone storage or a cloud drive you select) is under your control and subject to that service's own privacy policy. Android's own device backup may also include the app's data if you have it turned on; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.

Health Connect and Samsung Health (optional)

Lift Tracker can share data with Health Connect, Android's on-device health data store, which apps like Samsung Health and Google Fit also use. This is off until you tap Connect in Settings and allow access on Health Connect's permission screen. Data moves only between Lift Tracker and Health Connect on your phone; Lift Tracker still has no Internet access and nothing is sent to us.

  • Exercise (write) – each finished workout as an exercise session: start and end time, its name, the type "strength training", one segment per set with its repetitions, and notes listing the exercises, weights, reps and total volume.
  • Weight (write) – body-weight entries you log in Lift Tracker.
  • Weight (read) – only if Import weigh-ins is on: weigh-ins saved by other apps or a smart scale, starting from 30 days before you connect, so they appear in your body-weight log. Entries that match one you already logged are skipped.

When you edit or delete a workout or weigh-in in Lift Tracker, the matching record Lift Tracker wrote to Health Connect is updated or deleted. Lift Tracker does not read any other health data (such as heart rate, steps or sleep).

Data in Health Connect is controlled by you: you choose which apps may read or write each type, and you can revoke Lift Tracker's access or delete the data it wrote at any time in Health Connect (Settings → Health Connect → App permissions → Lift Tracker) or by turning off sync in Lift Tracker. Other apps, including Samsung Health, can see this data only if you allow them in Health Connect; their use of it is covered by their own privacy policies.

Health data obtained through Health Connect is used only to show and keep your workout and body-weight log in the app. It is never used for advertising, never sold, never used for credit or insurance decisions, and never transferred to us or to any third party. Lift Tracker's use of information received from Health Connect follows Google Play's Health Connect permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Permissions

  • Notifications – to show the rest-timer countdown and tell you when rest is over.
  • Vibrate – to buzz when the rest timer ends.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) – so the rest-timer alert fires on time while the screen is off.
  • Health Connect: write exercise, write weight, read weight – only if you turn on sync, as described above.

None of these permissions are used to collect or share information.

Sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer any data, because we do not receive any. The only time data leaves Lift Tracker is when you choose to: exporting a file, or turning on Health Connect sync, which keeps it on your phone in Health Connect under your control.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
