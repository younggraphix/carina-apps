Carina Notes – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · App: Carina Notes (com.carina.notes) · Developer: Carina Apps

Carina Notes is a notes and checklists app for Android. We do not collect any data.

Information we collect

None. Carina Notes has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device.

Information stored on your device

Your notes, checklists, color labels and settings are stored only in the app’s private storage on your phone. We never receive them. You can delete them at any time in the app (notes in the trash are deleted for good after 30 days, or right away with Empty trash / Delete forever), by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Home screen widget and shortcuts

The widget and the launcher shortcuts read and update your notes on the device. They do not send data anywhere. Anything the widget shows is visible on your home screen to anyone who can see your phone.

Export, import and sharing

If you choose Export notes, the app writes a JSON file to a location you pick with Android’s file picker. Import notes reads only the file you pick. If you use Share, the note’s text goes to the app you choose. Where exported or shared content goes afterwards (for example a cloud drive or messaging app) is under your control and covered by that service’s privacy policy.

Android’s own device backup may include the app’s data if you have it turned on; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
