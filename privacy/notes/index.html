Carina Notes – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026 · App: Carina Notes (com.carina.notes) · Developer: Carina Apps

Carina Notes is a notes and checklists app for Android. We do not collect any data. The only network use is Google Play Billing for the optional Pro purchase.

Information we collect

None. Carina Notes has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app never sends your notes or anything else off your device. Its Internet permission is added by Google Play Billing and is used only for the optional Pro purchase (see below).

Information stored on your device

Your notes, checklists, folders, color labels and settings are stored only in the app’s private storage on your phone. We never receive them. You can delete them at any time in the app (notes in the trash are deleted for good after 30 days, or right away with Empty trash / Delete forever), by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

App lock

If you turn on the app lock (a Pro feature), Android’s own screen-lock check (fingerprint, face, or your phone’s PIN, pattern or password) unlocks the app. The app never sees or stores your fingerprint, face data or PIN; it only learns whether the check passed. While the lock is on, notes are hidden in the recent-apps screen and screenshots are blocked.

Optional Pro purchase (Google Play Billing)

Carina Notes is free to use. An optional one-time Carina Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play. When you buy or restore Pro, Google Play Billing handles the purchase: Google processes the payment and keeps your payment details and purchase record under the Google Privacy Policy. The app only asks Google Play whether Pro has been bought, and saves a yes/no on your device so Pro keeps working offline.

This is the only reason the app has the Internet permission (Google Play Billing adds it). The app itself collects no data and never sends your content anywhere. Carina Apps never receives your card or payment details. Google Play gives us the standard order record for a sale (such as the order number, date, price and country), as it does for every Play purchase.

Home screen widget and shortcuts

The widget and the launcher shortcuts read and update your notes on the device. They do not send data anywhere. Anything the widget shows is visible on your home screen to anyone who can see your phone.

Export, import and sharing

If you choose Export notes, the app writes a JSON file to a location you pick with Android’s file picker. Import notes reads only the file you pick. If you use Share, the note’s text goes to the app you choose. Where exported or shared content goes afterwards (for example a cloud drive or messaging app) is under your control and covered by that service’s privacy policy.

Android’s own device backup may include the app’s data if you have it turned on; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
